## What I do

- **Ship end-to-end** — from blank repo to App Store and Play Store, including auth, monetization,
analytics, crashlytics, performance monitoring and store compliance.
- **Architecture-first React Native** — Expo Router for type-safe file-based routing, Reanimated worklets for 60fps interactions on the JS-thread limit, the New Architecture for direct native interop, and TypeScript end-to-end. I keep apps current across major RN, React and Expo upgrades without breaking production.
- **Production integrations, not just SDKs** — auth flows (Apple & Google Sign-In, biometrics), monetization (IAP, AdMob), observability (Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, Performance Monitoring), search and data (Firestore, Algolia InstantSearch), and lifecycle hooks (push, in-app review). Wired up to ship, not to "be on the resume".
